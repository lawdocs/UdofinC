## 2. Writing standard

- Plain English, active voice, short sentences, present tense, imperative for the coding agent ("Build…", "Reject…"). British/Indian spelling (organisation, licence, authorise). No marketing fluff. No emojis.
- The reader is a coding agent (Claude Code) plus the owner. Be specific: tables, field lists, state machines, settings keys, events, error codes, Gherkin. Prefer one precise table over three paragraphs.
- **Legal facts:** cite instrument + exact provision (e.g., "IT Act 2000, First Schedule, entry 1 (as amended 26 Sep 2022)"). Tag: **[LAW]** statute/regulator text, **[INTERP]** our reading, **[REC]** recommendation. Any unverified fact gets **[VERIFY: what to check, where]**. Never invent a section, circular, form number, fee, date or vendor API. When you rely on research, add the research ID (e.g., "(R3 LG-021)").
- Treat competitor and vendor claims as self-published; never copy their text.
- Mark items needing a lawyer: **[COUNSEL]**; needing a tax memo: **[TAX MEMO]**; owner decision: **[OWNER]**.
- Length targets: phase files 7,000–11,000 words; appendices as stated in the allocation. Quality over volume — no padding or repetition of the master prompt.
