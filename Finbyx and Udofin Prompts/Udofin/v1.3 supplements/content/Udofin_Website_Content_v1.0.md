### 4.2 How it works (`/how-it-works`) — legal review

**H1:** How Udofin works
**Lead:** Four steps. At each one you can stop, ask why, and take your time.

**Step 1 — Tell us what you need.** You register with your mobile number and tell us the loan type and amount. Before we ask for anything, we tell you why we need it, which lenders will receive it and how long it is kept. You give consent for each purpose separately. *[i: consent] [i: purpose_of_data]*

**Step 2 — Lenders check and make offers.** Your partner lenders check your details under their own rules. Some will match; some will not, and we tell you which did not and why. Offers appear side by side, sorted by APR, lowest first. Udofin does not decide anything here; the lenders do. *[i: the_lender_decides] [i: unmatched_lender] [i: ranking_metric]*

**Step 3 — Read the KFS, accept, sign.** Each offer comes with a Key Facts Statement from the lender: the amount, the APR, every charge, the schedule and what happens if you pay late. We explain every line and read it aloud if you want. If you accept, you sign electronically, and your lender confirms. *[i: kfs] [i: apr] [i: esign]*

**Step 4 — Repay your lender.** You set up a mandate or pay by UPI directly to your lender's account. Udofin shows you your lender's figures, with the time they were last updated. If you change your mind within the cooling-off period, you can exit on the terms the KFS states. *[i: pay_to_lender_only] [i: cooling_off_period]*

**What you will need:** your mobile number, your PAN, an identity document the lender accepts (for example through DigiLocker), and a bank account in your name. Your lender may ask for more; we will tell you why.

**The lender decides (callout):** Udofin is a Lending Service Provider. We carry your information to lenders and bring their decisions and offers back to you. We never approve, reject, price or rank for money.

### 4.4 Products (`/products`) — legal review

**H1:** Loan types on Udofin
**Lead:** Here is what each loan type is, who usually uses it and what a lender will typically ask for. Offers, rates and eligibility come from lenders, not from Udofin.
**Cards (neutral; no rates):**
- *Personal loan* — An unsecured loan for personal needs, repaid in monthly instalments. Lenders check your income and credit history.
- *Short-tenure loan* — A smaller loan repaid over a short period. Where a product repays in full within 60 days it is available on the web only, because app-store rules do not allow it in the apps. *[i: store_filtered_offer]*
- *Loan against securities* — A loan secured on shares or mutual-fund units you pledge. Your lender sets the eligible list and margins.
- *Home loan and loan against property* — Secured on property; longer tenor; the lender's valuation and legal checks apply.
- *Vehicle loan* — Secured on the vehicle.
- *Business loan* — For proprietors and small businesses; the lender will ask for business documents and GST details.
- *Microfinance (group) loans* — Offered by NBFC-MFIs to members of a group; household income rules apply by regulation.
- *Education, gold and other loans* — Offered where a partner lender provides them.
**Footer line:** Availability depends on which partner lenders offer a product in your state and on app-store rules. Udofin shows you every matching offer and names the lenders that did not match.

### 4.5 Safety & your data (`/safety`) — legal review; required blocks

**H1:** Safety and your data
**Lead:** Here is exactly what Udofin can and cannot do on your phone, where your data lives, how to check the app is genuine, and how to complain.

**Udofin's role.** Udofin is a Lending Service Provider under the Reserve Bank of India's digital-lending rules. We work for regulated lenders. The lender decides your application; Udofin never does. *[i: lsp] [i: the_lender_decides]*

**What we never access (table):** Contacts — never · SMS and messages — never · Call logs and phone state — never · Files, photos and media library — never (you pick a document through the system picker; we never browse) · Installed apps — never · Precise location — never, except inside a lender's video-KYC session if the lender requires it · Microphone — only if you choose voice help or video-KYC, while you use it.
**What we may ask for, and when:** Camera — at the KYC step, once, with your consent · Approximate location — once at onboarding, with your consent · Notifications — so your lender's service messages reach you; you choose which.
**Why this matters:** The RBI's rules bar lending apps from accessing contacts, call logs, files and media. If any loan app asks for these, do not install it, and report it. *[i: no_contacts_access] [i: rbi_sachet]*

**Where your data lives.** In India. Lenders hold your KYC and loan records; Udofin keeps only the minimum needed to run your journey, for the period our agreement with your lender allows, and then deletes it. You can see what we hold, who received it, and download it, any time, in the app under Account › Documents and data. *[i: data_in_india] [i: minimal_data] [i: download_my_data]*

**Your Key Facts Statement.** Before you accept any loan you receive a KFS from the lender: the amount you will get, the APR, every charge, the schedule and the penal charges. We explain each line, in your language, and read it aloud if you like. → Sample KFS, annotated *(resource)*

**Cooling-off.** After your loan is disbursed you have a cooling-off period, set by your lender and shown as a plain date. If you exit within it, you pay only what the KFS states for that case. There is no timer and no pressure. *[i: cooling_off_period]*

**How to complain.** 1 Raise it in the app under Help; it goes to your lender's grievance officer within an hour. 2 Your lender has 30 days to reply. 3 If the lender has an Internal Ombudsman, a rejected complaint goes there automatically. 4 If you are not satisfied, or 30 days pass, you may complain to the RBI Ombudsman within 90 days through the RBI's Complaint Management System. The app shows these clocks and never files for you. For matters about Udofin itself, contact our nodal grievance officer below. *[i: escalation_chain] [i: rbi_ombudsman]*

**Is this loan app safe? (health check).** Answer six questions and we tell you what to look for: Is the lender named on the RBI's list? Did the app ask for contacts, SMS or call logs? Did anyone ask for an advance fee? Is there a Key Facts Statement before you accept? Is there a cooling-off period? Is a grievance officer named? *(Educational only; no data stored; no lead capture.)*

**Check this app is genuine.** Udofin on Google Play: package `[package id]`; on the App Store: `[app id]`; the web app at `app.udofin.com`. Check our partner lenders on the RBI's list of digital lending apps. Udofin never asks you to install an APK from a link. → RBI Sachet to report a fraud.

**Udofin nodal grievance officer:** [Name], [designation], [email], [phone], Monday–Friday 10:00–18:00 IST, [address].

## 7. FAQ bank (48)

**Udofin and lenders**
1. **Is Udofin a lender?** No. Udofin is a Lending Service Provider. Loans come from regulated lenders named on every screen, and they decide your application.
2. **Who decides whether I get a loan?** The lender. Udofin carries your information to lenders and brings their decisions back. We never approve, reject or price a loan.
3. **Which lenders are on Udofin?** See the Lenders page. Each lender's disclosure page and grievance officer are linked there.
4. **Can I check Udofin is genuine?** Yes. Our partner lenders report Udofin's apps on the RBI's list of digital lending apps. Our store listings and package ids are on the Safety page.
5. **Does Udofin charge me anything?** No. Never. The RBI's rules say the lender pays a lending service provider, not the borrower.
6. **Then how does Udofin make money?** Lenders pay us under a lending service provider agreement. What a lender pays never affects how offers are matched or sorted.
7. **Will I be charged a convenience or platform fee at payment?** No. You pay your lender directly; Udofin adds nothing.
8. **Are there paid plans?** Not for anything loan-related. If we ever offer optional money tools, they will be separate, open to everyone at the same price, never required for a loan and never auto-renewed from a trial.

**Offers and the KFS**
9. **How are offers sorted?** By APR, lowest first; ties by monthly repayment. The rule is published on our site and cannot be bought.
10. **Why do I see lenders that did not match?** Because the RBI's rules and our own say you should know who looked and why they did not offer.
11. **What is a Key Facts Statement?** A one-page statement from the lender with the amount you receive, the APR, every charge, the schedule and the penal charges. You get it before you accept anything.
12. **What is APR?** The annual percentage rate: the total cost of the loan — interest and charges — expressed as a yearly rate so offers can be compared. We show the formula.
13. **Can I read the KFS in my language?** Yes, in twelve languages, with read-aloud. Amounts are shown in words too.
14. **How long is an offer valid?** Your lender sets a validity period; we show it as a plain date, never a timer.
15. **Why is there no "recommended" offer?** Because recommending would mean deciding for you. We show the facts and the sort rule.
16. **Why is a product shown as "available on the web only"?** App-store rules do not allow some short-tenure products in apps. The web shows everything the lender offers.

**Cooling-off, signing and paying**
17. **What is the cooling-off period?** A period after disbursal, set by your lender, in which you can exit the loan by repaying the principal and the proportionate APR as the KFS states. We show the end date plainly.
18. **How do I exit during cooling-off?** From your loan screen: "Exit this loan". We show what you pay and your lender confirms.
19. **How do I sign?** Electronically, with an Aadhaar-based eSign or another method your lender accepts, after an extra verification step on your phone.
20. **Where does my repayment go?** To your lender's account, by mandate or UPI. Udofin never holds your money.
21. **Why does a payment show "pending"?** Your lender's system confirms payments; until it does we show an honest status rather than a guess.
22. **What happens if I pay late?** The penal charges in your KFS apply, set by your lender. We never add anything.
23. **Will Udofin call me if I am late?** No. Reminders and recovery contact come from your lender or its appointed agent, within the RBI's rules on hours and conduct. You can verify an agent in the app.

**Data and privacy**
24. **What can Udofin see on my phone?** Only what you allow: the camera at the KYC step and an approximate location once at onboarding. Never contacts, SMS, call logs, files or installed apps.
25. **Where is my data stored?** In India.
26. **What does Udofin keep?** Only the minimum needed to run your journey, for the period our agreement with your lender allows. Your lender holds your KYC and loan records.
27. **Can I withdraw consent?** Yes, from Account › Documents and data, in fewer taps than giving it. We tell you what stops working if you do.
28. **Can I download my data?** Yes, any time, from the same place.
29. **Who else receives my data?** Your lender(s), the verification and signing providers named in the privacy policy, and nobody else. The list is public.
30. **Does Udofin share my data with other Infrabyx products?** No, not without your separate consent.
31. **Do you use my data to train AI?** No. Our AI providers are contractually barred from training on Udofin data, and regulated data is processed only in India with zero retention.

**Complaints**
32. **How do I complain about my loan?** In the app under Help. It reaches your lender's grievance officer within an hour.
33. **How long does the lender have?** 30 days. We show the clock.
34. **What if I am not satisfied?** If the lender has an Internal Ombudsman, a rejected complaint goes there automatically. After 30 days, or if you are unhappy with the reply, you can complain to the RBI Ombudsman within 90 days. We show you when and how; we never file for you.
35. **How do I complain about Udofin itself?** Contact our nodal grievance officer (Grievance page) or use Help › Raise a complaint and choose "About Udofin".
36. **Can I take a dispute to mediation?** Where your lender has agreed to online dispute resolution and refers your case, you can join it from inside the app. Equator Resolution, the platform used, is also an Infrabyx product; it is run separately and its staff have no role in Udofin.

**Safety**
37. **How do I spot a fake loan app?** It asks for contacts, SMS or call logs; it wants an advance fee; there is no lender named or no Key Facts Statement; there is no grievance officer; it is not on the RBI's list. Use our health check on the Safety page.
38. **Will Udofin ever ask me to install an APK from a link?** No.
39. **Why are screenshots blocked on some screens?** To protect your KYC, offer, KFS and payment details.
40. **What is device binding?** Your account is tied to your phone so that someone with your OTP alone cannot use it.

**Accessibility and languages**
41. **Which languages does Udofin support?** English, Hindi and ten more Indian languages for the borrower journey, with read-aloud.
42. **I find reading hard. Can I still use Udofin?** Yes. Easy-read mode is on by default: one question per screen, icons with labels, read-aloud, amounts in words, picture tutorials. You can also use assisted mode with a helper while keeping your OTP to yourself.
43. **I am deaf or hard of hearing.** Every video has captions; voice help has live captions and text; alerts are visual; every phone route has a chat or email alternative.
44. **Does Udofin work on a slow network?** Yes. Lite mode switches on automatically.

**Vidhi, the assistant**
45. **What is Vidhi?** An AI assistant that explains your loan, your KFS, your statements and your complaint options in your language. It is not a person and it never decides anything.
46. **Will Vidhi tell me which loan to take?** No. It explains terms and shows facts; it never recommends borrowing or a lender.
47. **Can I talk to a person instead?** Always. Say "talk to a person" at any point.
48. **Can I delete my conversations?** Yes, from Account › Settings › AI assistant.
