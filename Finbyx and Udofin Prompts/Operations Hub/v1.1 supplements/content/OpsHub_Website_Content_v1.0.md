## 23. FAQ bank (44)

**About the group**
1. **What is Infrabyx?** A group of Indian software products and services for regulated businesses and legal professionals, plus an independent dispute-resolution institution and a training arm.
2. **Is Infrabyx a bank, NBFC or lender?** No. Infrabyx companies build software and provide services. Udofin is a lending service provider; loans come from regulated lenders. Finbyx is software operated by its clients, who remain the regulated entities.
3. **Is Infrabyx a law firm?** No. LawDocs provides document technology and eSign. Professionals on group directories are independent and are listed in line with Bar Council of India rules.
4. **How are the group companies related?** Each product is a separate application with its own database, administration and release cycle. Shared services run under written intra-group agreements and data-processing agreements.
5. **Where are you based?** Delhi (registered office), Noida and Jaipur, with staff working remotely across India.
6. **Which legal entity am I contracting with?** The company named in your proposal and contract; the footer entity line and every invoice state the legal name, CIN and GSTIN.

**Products**
7. **Who is Finbyx for?** NBFCs, housing finance companies, co-operative lenders, ARCs and similar institutions that want staff-operated origination, servicing, accounting, recovery and returns in one system.
8. **Does Finbyx make credit decisions?** No. Finbyx records and supports decisions your staff take under your policies.
9. **Does Udofin lend money?** No. Udofin connects borrowers with regulated lenders and never holds funds or decides a loan.
10. **Who is LawDocsComply for?** Compliance, secretarial and risk teams at RBI-, SEBI- and IFSCA-regulated entities and listed companies.
11. **Can LawDocs sign documents for me?** LawDocs provides eSign workflows; the signatory is always you or your counterparty.
12. **What does Equator Resolution do?** It administers conciliation, mediation and arbitration online and facilitates Lok Adalat referrals, under published rules and fees.
13. **Is Equator independent of Infrabyx's lender clients?** Yes. Equator is a separate entity, its neutrals are not selected or paid by lenders, and no other group company can see its case files or outcomes.
14. **What does LawDocs Trainings offer?** Practical courses for compliance, secretarial, lending and legal-operations teams. Certificates record completion.
15. **Do you have pricing on this site?** No. Each product publishes its own pricing on its own site; group pages link to them.

**Security, hosting and trust**
16. **Where is my data hosted?** Production data for Indian clients is hosted in Mumbai with disaster recovery in Hyderabad. Development and staging use synthetic data only.
17. **Are you ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certified?** We publish only certifications actually issued, on the Trust Centre. Until then our programme is described as "in progress" with a target quarter.
18. **Do you run penetration tests?** Yes — vulnerability assessments every six months and penetration tests every twelve months by CERT-In-empanelled firms; summaries are on the Trust Centre and reports go to clients under NDA.
19. **Who are your sub-processors?** The current list, with purpose, region and DPA status, is on the Trust Centre; you can subscribe to change notices.
20. **Can my auditor or regulator see your documents?** Yes. Clients can download assurance documents from the Vendor Assurance Room with acknowledgement records, export evidence packs, and grant time-limited access to a named auditor or inspector.
21. **Do you share client data between group companies?** No, not without the client's written authorisation. Each product keeps its own database.
22. **How do you handle security incidents?** We follow CERT-In timelines, notify affected clients promptly under their contracts, and record incidents in our engineering console; details are on the Trust Centre.
23. **Do you use AI?** Yes — our AI assistant, Vidhi, drafts, summarises and answers questions with citations. It never makes credit, legal, hiring, pay or disciplinary decisions; named humans approve consequential actions. See the AI Use and Transparency policy.

**Privacy and consent**
24. **How do you use cookies?** Necessary cookies always; functional, analytics and marketing only with your consent. Change your choice any time from "Cookie settings".
25. **Will you send me marketing?** Only if you opt in separately. Every message has an unsubscribe link, and we honour Global Privacy Control and the national Do Not Disturb preferences where they apply.
26. **How do I make a data request?** Write to the Data Protection Officer named in the footer. We handle access, correction and erasure requests under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable rules.
27. **Do you message on WhatsApp?** Only after you opt in for that channel, and only with approved templates. We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN, card or bank details on WhatsApp.
28. **Who is your Grievance Officer?** Named, with e-mail and postal address, in the footer and on the Grievance page.

**Working with us**
29. **How do I become a client?** Use the contact form or the product site. You will get a named lead, a proposal from the right legal entity, and a contract with a data-processing agreement before any production use.
30. **Do you offer trials?** Products may offer sandbox or trial access. Trials never convert to a paid plan automatically; you will be told before a trial ends and asked to place an order.
31. **How does implementation work?** Through a project with milestones, a RAID log and a client-visible status page; data migration is reconciled before go-live and signed off by you.
32. **How does support work?** In-product support with severity-based response times set in your contract; borrower or consumer complaints about a lender go to that lender's grievance process, which we help route.
33. **Can we leave?** Yes. Every product provides a documented export in a standard archive format, and the exit process is in your contract.
34. **Do you work with partners?** Yes — implementation, technology, channel and training partners. See Partners.

**Careers**
35. **How do you work?** From three offices and remotely under a clear arrangement: declared sessions, agreed outcomes, equipment and allowances, and no keystroke, screenshot or webcam monitoring.
36. **Do you take interns?** Yes — structured programmes with a learning plan, a mentor, weekly check-ins, a stipend and a verifiable certificate.
37. **What happens to my application data?** It is used to consider your application and deleted after 12 months unless you join the talent pool.
38. **Do you accommodate disabilities?** Yes. Tell us what you need at any stage.

**Site and accessibility**
39. **Is this site accessible?** We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA and IS 17802 and publish known issues on the Accessibility page.
40. **Is the site available in Hindi?** Key pages are available in Hindi; use the language switch in the footer.
41. **How do I report a problem with the site?** Use "Report a problem" in the footer; you will receive a reference and status updates.
42. **Why is there no "certified" badge on your site?** Because we show badges only for certifications actually issued, with the issuer and scope.
43. **Can I republish your resources?** Resources are for your use under the Resources Download Terms; ask us before republishing.
44. **How do I contact security?** Through the responsible-disclosure page and `/.well-known/security.txt`.
