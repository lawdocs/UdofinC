## 24. FAQ bank (44)

**General**
1. **Is Finbyx a lender or an NBFC?** No. Finbyx is software. Regulated lenders use it to run their operations and remain responsible for their products and decisions.
2. **Who owns the data?** The lender (our customer). Finbyx processes it under a data processing agreement and never uses it for any other purpose.
3. **Where is data hosted?** In India, with primary and disaster-recovery regions. Non-production environments hold synthetic data only.
4. **Can we leave?** Yes. Full export in documented, human-readable formats, no erasure during transition, cooperation with your next provider, and a purge certificate.
5. **Do you stop access if we miss a payment?** Regulated functions, read access and export never stop. Dunning follows a notified path.
6. **Which lenders use Finbyx?** Named only with written permission. Ask us for references.

**Compliance and regulation**
7. **Is Finbyx RBI-approved?** RBI does not approve software vendors. Finbyx is designed to support compliance with the applicable RBI Directions; your institution remains the regulated entity.
8. **Does Finbyx file our returns?** No. It prepares, validates, reconciles and evidences them; your authorised officer files, and Finbyx records the acknowledgement.
9. **Do you store our CIMS or portal credentials?** Never. No portal credential, OTP, DSC token or PIN is stored or scripted.
10. **How do you handle a new RBI circular?** It becomes a dated rule-pack change with an impact report; regulatory floors update by release and cannot be relaxed by a tenant.
11. **Can a user override a regulatory floor?** No. Floors are enforced server-side; a tenant may only tighten.
12. **Bureau reporting cadence?** Built in for the current regulatory cadence across all four bureaus, with rejection repair and dispute handling.
13. **Does it support the microfinance rules?** Yes — household affordability, group lifecycles, call windows and MFI bureau format.
14. **Co-operative societies?** Yes, with their vocabulary, registers and guard-rails; they are not treated as NBFCs.

**Security**
15. **Are you ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certified?** See the Security & Trust page for the current status of each standard; we label anything in progress as such and never claim a certificate we do not hold.
16. **How is tenant data isolated?** API, service-layer and database (row-level security) isolation, tested each release.
17. **Can we use our own keys?** Yes, on enterprise plans (tenant-held keys, including external key stores).
18. **Can we use our own storage?** Yes — exports and backups to storage you control in India.
19. **How do you tell us about incidents?** Target within two hours of our detection for material events; the regulatory clock runs from our detection, and we support it.
20. **Penetration testing?** Independent assessments every six months and penetration tests annually by CERT-In-empanelled testers; summaries in your assurance room.
21. **Do staff see our data?** Only through logged break-glass access that you can see.

**Product**
22. **Which products can we originate?** Personal, MSME, LAP, home, vehicle, gold, LAS/LAMF, co-lending and specialist templates; your own templates through the product factory.
23. **Does it integrate with our core banking or Tally?** Yes — GL exports, Tally agent, SAP connectors; others through the integration hub.
24. **Offline field work?** Yes — visits, documents, receipts and PTP offline with safe sync; sanctions and disbursements never offline.
25. **Multi-entity groups?** Yes — legal entities, branches and products with consolidated views by permission.
26. **Can we attach the Recovery Suite later?** Yes; and detach it later with records kept read-only.
27. **Languages?** Staff UI in English (Hindi optional); borrower documents in the borrower's preferred Eighth Schedule language as template packs are approved.
28. **Accessibility?** WCAG 2.2 AA with captions, read-aloud and a low-literacy field mode.

**AI (Vidhi)**
29. **What is Vidhi?** The Infrabyx AI assistant inside Finbyx. It summarises, drafts and explains for professionals.
30. **Can Vidhi approve a loan?** No. Credit decisions, pricing, disbursement, borrower contact, write-offs, notices, filings and KYC approvals are locked for AI at every level.
31. **Does Vidhi contact borrowers?** Never. It can plan tasks for your collectors; humans contact.
32. **Where does AI inference run?** On India-resident endpoints with zero vendor retention for any borrower data; banks may bring their own approved endpoint.
33. **Is our data used to train models?** No.
34. **Can we switch Vidhi off?** Yes, per tenant, per skill, or entirely.

**Commercial**
35. **How is Finbyx priced?** By plan and module from our catalogue; see Pricing. Prices shown come from the signed catalogue.
36. **Is there a trial?** A sandbox tenant with synthetic data. Trials never convert automatically.
37. **Who invoices us?** The Infrabyx entity named in your agreement; GST invoices are issued.
38. **White-label?** Yes — brand packs for lenders with several brands and for resellers; regulatory disclosures keep their locked text.
39. **Implementation time?** Depends on products, migration and integrations; a typical pilot runs in defined phases with sign-offs. [OWNER: typical range only with evidence]
40. **Support hours?** Monday–Saturday 09:30–18:30 IST; incident response per SLA.

**Website and privacy**
41. **What cookies do you use?** Necessary by default; functional, analytics and marketing only with consent.
42. **Who is your Grievance Officer?** Named in the footer and on /grievance.
43. **Can I write a guest post?** Verified professionals and customer administrators can submit; posts are moderated and carry a disclosure.
44. **Do you accept vulnerability reports?** Yes — see Security & Responsible Disclosure and `security.txt`.
