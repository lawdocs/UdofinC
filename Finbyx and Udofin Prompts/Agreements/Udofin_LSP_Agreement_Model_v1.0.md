## RECITALS

(A) The RE is a non-banking financial company registered with the Reserve Bank of India ("**RBI**") that extends credit, including through Digital Lending, under its Board-approved credit policy.

(B) The LSP operates the Udofin platform (Android and iOS apps, a web application, interfaces and portals), through which it sources, onboards and services borrowers for several regulated entities. Each Udofin app and web journey is a Digital Lending App (CF para 4(1)(x)).

(C) A lending service provider is "an agent of an NBFC ... who carries out one or more of NBFC's digital lending functions, or part thereof, in customer acquisition, services incidental to underwriting and pricing, servicing, monitoring, recovery of specific loan or loan portfolio on behalf of the NBFC in conformity with extant outsourcing guidelines" (CF para 4(1)(xix)).

(D) Digital lending through an LSP must be carried out under a contract that clearly defines the roles, rights and obligations of each party (CF para 6(1)). Such a contract does not dilute the RE's obligations, and the RE remains fully responsible and liable for all acts and omissions of the LSP (CF para 6(7)).

(E) The arrangement is outsourcing of financial services under Chapter III of the OUT Directions (OUT paras 4(1), 58(3)(iii)) and, to the extent the LSP hosts systems or data for the RE, may also be IT outsourcing under Chapter IV (OUT paras 5(1), 58(2)), as the RE assesses.

(F) The RE has carried out enhanced due diligence on the LSP (CF para 6(2); OUT paras 29–32, 71) and obtained the approvals required by its outsourcing policy.

(G) The LSP has agreements with more than one lender, so CF para 7 applies to the display of offers on the Udofin DLAs.

(H) The RE wishes to appoint the LSP, on a non-exclusive basis, to perform the LSP Functions. The Parties may also agree recovery-agent services or a Default Loss Guarantee, but only by executing Schedule 10 or Schedule 11.

# 13. DEFAULT LOSS GUARANTEE (OPTIONAL)

**13.1 Only by DLG Agreement.** [Option] The LSP provides DLG only under a DLG Agreement in the form of Schedule 11, an explicit and legally enforceable contract stating at least the extent of cover, the form in which it is maintained with the RE, the invocation timeline and the disclosure requirements (CF para 22).

**13.2 Eligibility.** [Mandatory — RBI] The RE may enter into DLG only with an LSP (or another lender engaged as an LSP) that is incorporated as a company under the Companies Act, 2013 (CF para 19).

**13.3 Policy and declaration.** [Mandatory — RBI] Before any DLG, the RE's policy shall set the eligibility criteria for DLG providers, the nature and extent of cover, monitoring and review, and the fees payable to the provider (CF para 20(1)). This is additional to Article 4 (CF para 20(4)).

**13.4 Excluded loans.** [Mandatory — RBI] No DLG on Loans covered by credit guarantee schemes run by trust funds (CF para 21(1)); guarantees under CF para 29 are not DLG.

**13.5 Forms.** [Mandatory — RBI] DLG may be held only as cash deposited with the RE, a fixed deposit with a Scheduled Commercial Bank with lien marked in favour of the RE, or a bank guarantee in favour of the RE (CF para 23).

**13.6 Cap and DLG Set.** [Mandatory — RBI] Total DLG cover on any outstanding portfolio specified upfront shall not exceed five per cent of the amount disbursed out of that portfolio at any time; for implicit guarantees the LSP shall not bear performance risk beyond the equivalent of five per cent of the underlying portfolio (CF para 24(1)). The portfolio shall consist of identifiable, measurable sanctioned loans (the "**DLG Set**"), which stays fixed and is not dynamic (CF para 24(2)).

**13.7 Implicit guarantees count.** [Mandatory — RBI] Any arrangement linked to portfolio performance and specified upfront is DLG, by whatever name called (CF para 4(1)(viii)). The following are therefore DLG, count towards the Clause 13.6 cap and are valid only if documented in the DLG Agreement and satisfied solely from cover held in a form permitted by Clause 13.5 (CF para 23), never by withholding, netting or set-off of Fees (Schedule 1, para 1.9; Schedule 11, para 11.6); otherwise they are void: (a) any clawback, reversal, refund, holdback, deferral or reduction of Fees by reference to delinquency, default, loss or write-off; (b) any first-loss, loss-sharing, top-up or make-whole commitment; (c) any obligation to buy back defaulted Loans (which is prohibited in any event, because the LSP is not a permitted transferee under TDCR para 12(8) and a buy-back is not a CF para 23 form); and (d) any deposit, security or set-off right of the RE tied to portfolio performance. Fees based only on disbursement volume are not DLG.

**13.8 Invocation and tenor.** [Mandatory — RBI] (a) The RE shall invoke DLG within a maximum overdue period of 120 days, unless the Borrower makes good the dues earlier (CF para 27(1)). (b) The DLG Agreement shall run for not less than the longest tenor of the Loans in the DLG Set (CF para 27(2)). (c) Invoked DLG shall not be reinstated, including through recovery (CF para 25(4)). (d) Invoked DLG is not set off against individual Loans and Borrower liability is unaffected (CF para 25(2)); the DLAs shall never show a Loan as reduced by invocation. (e) Recoveries on Loans where DLG was invoked and realised may be shared as the DLG Agreement provides (CF para 25(3)).

**13.9 Prudential treatment.** [Mandatory — RBI] The RE classifies Loans regardless of DLG and provisions them under the RBI (NBFC – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Directions, 2025 (CF para 25(1)), which permit an RE applying Ind AS to consider the DLG in Expected Credit Loss provisioning and require ECL to be recomputed after each invocation (paras 36A–36C of those Directions); capital treatment follows the capital-adequacy Directions (CF para 26(1)). A compliant DLG is neither synthetic securitisation nor loan participation (CF para 30(2)).

**13.10 Monthly disclosure.** [Mandatory — RBI] The LSP shall publish on its website, monthly and within seven working days after each month ends, the total number of portfolios and the amount of each on which DLG has been offered (CF para 28), and send the RE evidence of each publication. Naming the RE is optional, as agreed in Schedule 11.

**13.11 No collection rights.** [Mandatory — RBI] DLG is loss-sharing only. It gives the LSP no right, duty or authority to collect, to contact Borrowers about dues, to influence the RE's recovery strategy, or to receive Borrower-level data beyond what is needed to validate invocations. Any recovery role arises only under Schedule 10. No DLG data shall be available to the matching, ranking, routing or collections functions of the Udofin Platform.

**13.12 Run-off.** On termination, DLG on each existing DLG Set continues for the Clause 13.8(b) period or until the set is repaid or written off, whichever is earlier, and Clause 13.10 continues for that period.
