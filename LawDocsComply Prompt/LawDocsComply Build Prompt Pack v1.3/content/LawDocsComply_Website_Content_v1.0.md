## 0. Rules this deck obeys

**Claims Register (NN-19; P23 §4D; IPB-04-07).** A claim is any number, percentage, time saving, count of customers or regulations, badge, award, comparison or statement about a third party. Each claim carries an evidence class: **E1** internal measurement with method and date; **E2** a public source (statute, regulator notification, published report) with URL and as-of date; **E3** a certificate or signed document we hold. A claim without a class cannot publish (`LDC-WEB-056` for the Investors page; the general claims lint for every page). The register entries this deck requires are listed in §30 with proposed classes; the content team attaches evidence before entry.

**Words we never use on the public site.** "RBI-approved", "RBI-compliant product", "regulator-endorsed", "ISO certified" (until a certificate exists — then with issuer, scope and validity), "SOC 2 certified" (SOC 2 is a report, not a certificate), "guaranteed", "guarantee", "100% secure", "fully compliant", "zero risk", "never miss a deadline" (as a promise), "AI that files for you", "legal advice", "the only", "the first", "the largest", "trusted by thousands" (without E1), "bank-grade" (undefined), "military-grade" (undefined). The Studio lint (`IPB-CMS-012`, `IPB-POL-003`) blocks the list; this deck was written against it.

**Boundary statement (appears on About, footer, Pricing FAQ, Health Check result).** "LawDocsComply provides compliance-management software and curated regulatory information. It does not provide legal, secretarial, tax or audit opinions. You remain responsible for your organisation's compliance and for every filing you make."

**DPDP and ASCI.** Every form states the purpose, links the relevant Privacy Notice clause and separates marketing consent (unticked). No pre-ticked boxes, no dark patterns, no countdown timers, no "only 3 seats left". Comparative statements about competitors appear only on Compare pages after legal review and are factual, dated and sourced (Appendix R). No copy in this deck is taken from any competitor's site.

**Voice.** Calm, exact, useful. We write like a good compliance officer speaks: short sentences, the regulation named, the date given, the action clear. We say "you" and "we". We prefer "shows", "tracks", "records", "cites" over "revolutionises", "empowers", "unlocks". We explain why something applies before we ask for anything.

## 27. FAQ bank (≥ 40; grouped; each answer stands alone and may be reused on any page; claims marked)

**About the product**

1. **Is LawDocsComply a law firm or a compliance consultancy?** No. LawDocsComply provides compliance-management software and curated regulatory information. It does not provide legal, secretarial, tax or audit opinions, and it does not file with any regulator. Where you need a professional, our Marketplace lists independent practitioners whose engagement is directly with you.
2. **What does "cited to the regulation" mean?** Every obligation in our library names the instrument (for example a master direction or regulation), the clause or paragraph, the issue date, the date it was last amended, the date our reviewer last checked it and the reviewer of record. You can open the source from the obligation.
3. **Which regulators and laws does the library cover?** RBI (commercial banks, small finance and payments banks, RRBs, urban and rural co-operative banks, NBFCs, ARCs and other regulated entities), SEBI (listed entities, mutual funds, AIFs, REITs and InvITs, intermediaries), IRDAI (insurers and intermediaries), IFSCA (regulated entities, finance companies and units, fund management entities), MCA (companies and LLPs), co-operative societies law (credit and other societies), and the overlays every entity faces: data protection, cyber security, labour codes and POSH, GST and direct tax, ESG and BRSR, AI governance. The published count per family is shown on each Solution page from the live registry [Claims: E1 registry count].
4. **Do you upload every RBI circular ever issued?** No. Our RBI content base is built on the consolidated directions the RBI issued on 28 November 2025 and the amendments and directions issued after that date, including the Department of Supervision returns directions of 31 July 2026 [Claims: E2 RBI press release and the LDC catalogue]. Older circulars that the consolidation superseded are not loaded as obligations.
5. **How do you know what applies to my entity?** You answer up to twelve questions (regulator, entity type, layer or tier, size, listing status, State, activities), or you let us pre-fill them from public registers through a licensed provider with your consent. The applicability engine then lists the instruments and obligations and shows the rule that makes each one apply. You confirm or correct before anything is created.
6. **What is a CompID?** A permanent, human-readable identifier the platform assigns to every compliance question when a direction's sheet is published — for example `RBI-NBFC-GOV25-0042`. It never changes and is never reused, so you can quote it in a board note, an audit or a reply to a regulator and everyone finds the same item.
7. **What are RiskIDs and GovIDs?** The same idea for risk items and governance items: stable identifiers that the audit trail, conversations, bookmarks and exports are keyed to.
8. **How are red, amber and green decided?** By a deterministic rule from the item's status, its next due date, its frequency window and its risk category. The same facts always give the same colour, and the rule is shown when you hover. A separate, labelled "at risk of slipping" prediction is kept apart from the RAG itself.
9. **Can we use our own terminology?** Yes. Co-operative societies see Managing Committee, Members and Annual General Body Meeting; LLPs see Designated Partners; you can override terms within limits. Legal disclaimers and statutory form layouts never change.
10. **Can we import our existing Excel trackers?** Yes. Upload your sheet; the importer proposes a mapping to our template contract, shows a preview, row errors and a diff, and nothing publishes until you approve. Imports are one change set with a 24-hour undo.

**Working the register**

11. **Who can mark an item as complied?** A maker records the work and attaches evidence; a checker approves; an auditor may test. No item is complied without a checker, and the AI assistant can never change a status.
12. **How does evidence work?** Evidence attaches to the compliance item (and to the CompID), is scanned for malware, versioned, stored immutably and shown with who uploaded it and when. Evidence requests to colleagues or vendors are tracked until fulfilled.
13. **What is the Work Inbox?** One list of what needs your action — approvals, evidence requests, overdue items, conversations awaiting your reply — ordered by urgency with a suggested next action. It works entirely from the keyboard.
14. **Can seniors ask for an explanation of a delay?** Yes. Each item has typed conversations: explanation requests, guidance requests, queries, notes and decisions, each with a response time. The accountability record shows who held an item, for how long and what it was waiting on, computed from the ledger. What an organisation does with that record is a matter for its own HR policy.
15. **Does the calendar sync to Outlook or Google Calendar?** Yes — Microsoft 365 (work and personal accounts), Google Calendar, and ICS subscriptions for Apple and others. Deadlines, meetings and reminders appear in your own calendar.
16. **Can an auditor get access?** Yes. Internal and external auditors use a read-only assurance view scoped to what you grant, with their own log. Board members use a board view for packs and minutes.

**Regulatory change**

17. **How quickly do you reflect a regulatory change?** Our systems scan regulator sources daily at 20:00 IST and flag changes; our content team reviews and publishes the impact — what changed, where, and which obligations are affected — and your register shows proposed re-mappings for your approval. We state the date of each review. We do not promise same-day coverage of every regulatory action.
18. **Will a change alter our register automatically?** No. Proposed re-mappings wait for a person in your organisation to accept them.

**Vidhi and AI**

19. **What is Vidhi?** The AI assistant built into LawDocsComply on the Infrabyx Vidhi AI kernel. It drafts, summarises, classifies, extracts, translates and answers questions from cited notebooks.
20. **Does Vidhi make decisions or file anything?** No. Vidhi never marks an obligation complied, never submits a return or filing, never changes an owner, never closes a finding, never publishes a policy and never sends anything to a regulator, auditor or counter-party. Those actions always need a person.
21. **Does the AI see our documents, and where are they processed?** Only the documents you point it at, only to produce the output you asked for, and — for confidential, financial, legal, HR and regulated data — only on India-resident endpoints under zero-retention contracts. Your content is never used to train models. The provider used is shown on each output.
22. **Can we turn Vidhi off or limit it?** Yes. Administrators can disable AI features for the organisation, tighten autonomy limits per skill, disable individual agents and schedules, and see usage and cost.
23. **Is AI output labelled?** Yes. Every output carries "AI-generated — review before use", the date and the model family. Images we generate for our own website carry a visible label and embedded provenance.
