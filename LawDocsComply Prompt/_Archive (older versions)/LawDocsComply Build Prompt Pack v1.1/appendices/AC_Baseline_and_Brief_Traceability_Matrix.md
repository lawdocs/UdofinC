## AC.1 How to read this matrix

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ID | `TM-nnn`, stable across versions; never reused |
| Source | Baseline document and section (Arch P1/P2/P3 = *LawDocs Comply Architecture* Parts 1–3; 'Earlier master prompt'; 'Owner brief') |
| Baseline item | The feature, form, register, screen, workflow or ask |
| Status | **COVERED** — specified at build depth in the pack; **PARTIAL** — present but thinner than the baseline (fields, steps, screens or rules missing); **MISSING** — not specified anywhere in the v1.0 pack (v1.1 re-audit status shown with *(v1.1)* or *(open after v1.1)*, §AC.0); **CHANGED-BY-DECISION** — deliberately replaced or narrowed by a management decision or NN rule (cited in Note) |
| Pack location(s) | Up to three locations with the strongest evidence (file and §/FR); located by search of the v1.0 pack and checked by the auditor |
| Absorb in (v1.1) | For PARTIAL/MISSING rows: the file that must absorb the gap (see `_contrib/v11/G5_gaps_by_file.md` for the specification to port) |
| Note | Why the status was given, or the decision that governs it |

**Method.** The baseline was converted to Markdown (pandoc) and decomposed by heading into 1,069 traceable items; repetitive sub-sections (per-regulator go-to-market chapters, per-regulation instrument lists, tutorial lists) were grouped. Each item was matched to the pack by targeted search patterns and the evidence was reviewed by the auditor; statuses for major modules were assigned by reading the pack sections. Pack files created in v1.1 (Appendices Y, Z, AA, AB, P25 and later) are not used as evidence here because they were in progress during the audit; rows they close are flagged in the Absorb column.
