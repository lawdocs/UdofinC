## J.1 Why this appendix exists

LawDocsComply drafts financial statements, notes, audit programmes, audit reports, minutes and registers for many entity types. Each must follow the accounting framework, the auditing and internal-audit standards, and the secretarial standards that apply to that entity in that year. The rules below are deterministic inputs to the P11 framework resolver and to the P10/P06 template packs; every output records the framework and standards versions it used.

## J.3 Resolver pseudo-code (P11 FR-P11-69)

```text
if regulator_class in {SCB, SFB, PB, LAB, RRB, UCB, StCB, DCCB}:
    fw = AS_BANK; fmt = BR_THIRD_SCHEDULE
    loss = (regulator_class == SCB and fy >= "2027-28") ? RBI_BANK_ECL_2026 : IRACP_BANK
elif regulator_class == IFSC_IBU: fw = IFRS; ccy = USD
elif regulator_class in {IFSCA_FC, IFSCA_FU, IFSCA_FME}: fw = PROFILE_SETTING  # [VERIFY]
elif regulator_class == IRDAI_INSURER: fw = IRDAI_FORMATS (Ind AS deferred)      # [VERIFY]
elif regulator_class in {SEBI_MF_SCHEME}: fw = SEBI_MF
elif regulator_class in {SEBI_REIT, SEBI_INVIT}: fw = IND_AS_SEBI
elif legal_form == COMPANY:
    if first_ind_as_year: fw = IND_AS                              # sticky
    elif is_nbfc (Rule 2(g) list incl. HFC, core investment co; ARC per [VERIFY]):
        if nw_2016 >= 500cr or group_link(PHASE_I): fw = IND_AS from FY2018-19
        elif listed or 250cr <= nw < 500cr or group_link(PHASE_II): fw = IND_AS from FY2019-20
        else: fw = AS_2021                                          # no voluntary adoption for NBFCs [VERIFY]
    else:
        if listed_main_board or nw >= 250cr or group_link(IND_AS): fw = IND_AS
        else: fw = AS_2021 (voluntary IND_AS allowed)
    fmt = IND_AS ? (is_nbfc ? SCH3_DIV3 : SCH3_DIV2) : SCH3_DIV1
elif legal_form == COOPERATIVE: fw = ACT_FORMAT + ICAI_AS(size)
elif legal_form in {LLP, PARTNERSHIP, TRUST, SOCIETY}: fw = ICAI_AS_NON_COMPANY(MSME|LARGE)
outputs: framework, currency, statement set, disclosure packs, CARO flag, AUD flag, s.204 flag,
         MCA route (AOC-4 / AOC-4 NBFC / XBRL / none), audit-trail (Rule 11(g)) flag
```

### J.4.3 Prudential overlays (never confuse with the accounting framework)
- **IRACP vs ECL for Ind AS NBFCs:** compute ECL (STAT) and IRACP (PRUD); shortfall appropriated to Impairment Reserve below PAT; release only with DoS permission; reserve excluded from capital and from net NPA (IRACP 34–36). FS 21(15) comparison table.
- **Ind AS 109 governance gates (FS 12(1))**: Board-approved business-model and ECL policies; management overlays with Audit Committee approval; SICR rebuttal above 30 DPD only with case file, impossible above 60 DPD; > 90 DPD not in Stage 3 needs ACB approval and disclosure.
- **Bank ECL from 1 Apr 2027** (RBI/DOR/2026-27/398): staging, floors (secured retail/corporate S1 0.40%, S2 5%; unsecured retail S1 1%, S2 5%; housing S1 0.25%, S2 1.5%; gold S1 0.40%, S2 1.5%; Stage 3 vintage floors to 100%), transition add-back factors 0.8/0.6/0.4/0.2 for FY 2027-28 to 2030-31 [VS Finbyx AC2.6].
- **Investment valuation:** banks (RBI investment portfolio directions; HTM/AFS/FVTPL framework from 1 Apr 2024 [VERIFY]); NBFCs (Ind AS 109/113 or AS 13 + RBI); insurers (IRDAI investment regulations).
LDC models these as **checks and disclosures** for non-Finbyx tenants (inputs supplied by the tenant), never as book-of-record computation.

### J.9A Co-operative audit and accounting standards

- **Statutory audit of co-operatives** is conducted by auditors on the panel maintained by the Registrar (State Acts) or the Central Registrar (MSCS Act 2002 s.70 as amended in 2023, read with the MSCS Rules 2023 [VERIFY section after amendment]); audit classification (A–D) and rectification reports follow the State Act or MSCS Rules (P16, Appendix S).
- **Accounting formats** are those prescribed by the governing Act and Registrar; ICAI Accounting Standards for non-company entities apply where the Act is silent [ICAI; VERIFY per State].
- **ICAI guidance**: ICAI's publications on audit of co-operative societies and co-operative banks [VERIFY current titles and editions] are used to seed programmes as recommendatory steps.
- **Banks among co-operatives** (UCBs, StCBs, DCCBs) follow the BR Act s.56 regime, RBI (or NABARD) directions and the Bank Guidance Note (§J.5), not the society formats.

## J.11 How exports follow the standards (binding output rules)

1. **Framework stamp.** Every statement, schedule and note export carries framework, Division/format, FY, rounding unit, mapping version and TB batch hashes in the document properties and a footer line.
2. **Comparatives always.** Statements include comparative period figures (Ind AS 1 / AS 1 / Schedule III); where unavailable, "not presented — first year" with reason.
3. **Rounding (Schedule III general instructions).** Turnover < ₹100 crore: nearest hundreds, thousands, lakhs or millions, or decimals thereof; ≥ ₹100 crore: nearest lakhs, millions or crores, or decimals thereof; one unit used uniformly; regulatory returns use the template's own unit (₹ crore/₹ lakh) — never mixed in one document.
4. **Mandatory disclosures cannot be deleted.** Schedule III Division-specific line items, additional regulatory information datasets (2021 amendment), RBI FS Directions tables (C.1/C.2/C.3), CARO-relevant data, BR Act Third Schedule schedules are Locked sections; "nil" requires evidence.
5. **Drafts are labelled.** "Draft — prepared by management; not audited" watermark until the Board-approval reference and auditor's report date are recorded; then "Approved by the Board on <date>" with signatory block.
6. **Open formats, Office-compatible.** DOCX (notes; tracked changes between versions), XLSX (statements with live formulas and a hidden lineage sheet), PDF/A-2b (approved), CSV (data), JSON (manifest) — all openable without LDC (NN-10; R-043).
7. **Folder layout** per Appendix M: `11 Returns and Regulatory Reporting/Accounts and Tax/<FY>/02 Financial statements/<Draft|Approved>/<Entity> – <Framework> – <Statements|Notes|Schedules>.<ext>`.
8. **Audit documentation exports** follow SA 230 / SIA 330 indexing (workpaper index references preserved in file names: `B.3.2 – Loan disbursement test.docx`).
9. **Secretarial exports** follow SS-1/SS-2 minute-book conventions (page numbering, initialling evidence, signing date) and MR-3 layout.
10. **Evidence integrity.** SHA-256 manifest and BSA 2023 s.63 certificate template (Part A by the tenant, Part B by an expert where required) accompany exports (NN-09).
11. **No reproduction of copyrighted standards text** (ICAI/ICSI/IASB) — citations and paraphrase only.

## J.16 Legal-matter accounting and disclosure (P26) *(v1.1)*

**Correction to the owner's baseline.** The baseline (Architecture Part 3, Table 23-1) maps litigation to Ind AS 37 using fixed percentage bands (probable ≥ 75%, possible 25–75%, remote < 25%). Ind AS 37 does not use those bands: an outflow is **probable** when it is *more likely than not* (paras 15–16, 23); a provision is recognised when there is a present obligation, a probable outflow and a reliable estimate (para 14); a **contingent liability** is disclosed when the outflow is not probable unless it is **remote** (paras 27–28, 86). AS 29 (revised) follows the same structure for AS entities. P26 therefore records a likelihood assessment with three classes (PROBABLE, POSSIBLE, REMOTE) and an optional percentage estimate; the class — not a fixed band — drives treatment; any tenant percentage bands are configurable guidance only and labelled as such. *(v1.1)*

| Item | Rule | Product effect |
|---|---|---|
| Provision | Present obligation + probable outflow + reliable estimate; best estimate; discounting where material (Ind AS 37 paras 36–47) | P26 provision assessment form; quarterly movement schedule to P11 |
| Contingent liability | Disclose nature, estimate of financial effect, uncertainties, possibility of reimbursement unless remote | P11 notes ("Claims against the company not acknowledged as debts") |
| Contingent asset | Disclose only when inflow probable; recognise only when virtually certain | P26 for claims by the company |
| Recovery exposures of lenders | Provisioning under RBI IRACP / ECL (Finbyx), not Ind AS 37 bands | Read-only projection in P26 (D-41, NN-39) |
| Schedule III | Contingent liabilities and commitments note (Division I/II/III) | P11 note builder |
| CARO 2020 | Disputed statutory dues not deposited (clause (vii)(b)) | P10 CARO pack reads P26 tax/statutory matters |
| SEBI LODR Reg 30 | Material litigation/dispute disclosure (Schedule III Part A Para B) within 24 hours [VERIFY] subject to materiality policy | P06 disclosure case created from P26 matter |
| RBI inspection annexures | Legal cases filed by/against the entity and orders against the entity or directors | P26 reports |
