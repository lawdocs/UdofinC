## D. Owner's live site (lawdocscomply.com), fetched 25 Oct 2026

- Every route (including `/`, `/pricing`, `/llms.txt`) returned only `<title>LawdocsComply</title>` and a viewport meta to a non-JS fetcher: the site is a client-rendered SPA and `/llms.txt` falls through to the SPA shell. Retrieval-based AI engines and non-rendering crawlers therefore see no content. **Finding for master: public site must be server-rendered/static (Next.js SSG/ISR) and llms.txt served as `text/plain` (P23 FR-P23-60).**
- sitemap.xml lists 15 URLs: /, /features, /pricing, /about, /bookdemo, /contact, /blog, /blog/financial-reporting-compliance-checklist, /updates, /privacy-policy, /terms, /cancellation-refund-policy, /shipping-policy, /customersupport, /careers. Existing slugs must be preserved or 301-redirected.

## F. SEO / GEO / AEO (2026)

- Google deprecated FAQ rich results: stopped appearing 7 May 2026; report/RRT support removed June 2026; Search Console API support ends Aug 2026. FAQPage markup is harmless but yields no rich result. (SEJ, https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-drops-faq-rich-results/574429/)
- HowTo rich results deprecated on mobile since Aug 2023.
- Retrieval engines cite pages they can fetch as raw HTML; ranking in classic search gates most citations; question-matching titles, natural slugs, answer-first sections, freshness (75% of cited pages updated within a year); earned third-party mentions dominate AI citations; llms.txt adoption high but rarely requested (useful mainly for docs). Crawler controls are per bot: OAI-SearchBot (ChatGPT search) vs GPTBot (training); Google-Extended does not affect AI Overviews inclusion; PerplexityBot; ClaudeBot/Claude-SearchBot. (Pepper 2026 guide, vendor blog — treat as indicative.)

## G. Accessibility (India + WCAG)

- RPwD Act 2016 s.40 (accessibility standards), s.42 (ICT access), s.46 (service providers to comply within two years of rules), s.89 (penalties). RPwD Rules 2017 r.15(1)(c) → IS 17802 (Part 1) 2021 and (Part 2) 2022.
- Rajive Raturi v Union of India (SC, 8 Nov 2024): r.15 largely recommendatory/ultra vires; Union to frame mandatory standards. Pragya Prasun (SC, 30 Apr 2025): accessible digital KYC alternatives.
- **Draft RPwD (Amendment) Rules 2026**, S.O. 3962(E) of 16 Jul 2026 (Gazette 20 Jul 2026): applies to any establishment making websites/apps/digital documents available in India; IS 17802 Parts 1–2; mandatory Accessibility Conformance Report (human + machine readable), re-test after major change, review ≥ every 2 years; 12 months (turnover ≥ ₹500 cr) or 18 months to comply, full within 2 years; s.89 fines; possible suspension of registrations. Status: draft [VERIFY notification].
- CCPD enforcement (2024–25): IAAP-certified audits ordered; fines ₹10,000 (Feb 2025, 155 establishments) and ₹50,000 (Jun 2025, 96).
- **SEBI circular 31 Jul 2025** (revised 29 Aug 2025): REs' digital platforms to comply with RPwD Act/r.15(1)(c), IS 17802, WCAG 2.1+, GIGW 3.0; annual IAAP-certified audit; accessible documents incl. ISL/captions; nodal officer; accessibility grievance channel; revised milestones — auditor by 14 Dec 2025, audit by 30 Apr 2026, remediation by 31 Jul 2026, annual audit/report by 30 Apr 2027 [VERIFY on sebi.gov.in]. **Product opportunity: an "Accessibility compliance" obligation pack + Marketplace IAAP audit service.**
- WCAG 2.2 (W3C Rec, Oct 2023) new AA criteria: 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), 2.5.7 Dragging Movements, 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum, 24×24 CSS px), 3.2.6 Consistent Help (A), 3.3.7 Redundant Entry (A), 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication (Minimum); 4.1.1 Parsing removed.
