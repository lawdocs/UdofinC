## O.0 Purpose and precedence

This appendix seeds the marketplace category tree and first-party offerings, defines provider verification, the three commercial models, the professional-conduct rules engine values, contract and refund templates, data-protection roles, the RE-buyer guard-rails and the independence wall for certification work. Appendix G (legal rules register) prevails on law; OH8 prevails on prices, tax and stream gates; OH9 prevails on directory credentials and Rule 36 fields.

## O.1 Marketplace principles

| # | Principle |
|---|---|
| MP-1 | Open to all: browse, search and read without login; buy services with or without a software subscription |
| MP-2 | LawDocs first: launch with first-party offerings; outside providers only after verification and profession clearance |
| MP-3 | Professional-conduct by design: the render layer, not staff discipline, prevents forbidden listings, ratings, fees and calls to action |
| MP-4 | Scope clarity: every offering states inclusions, **exclusions**, deliverables, inputs, timeline and price basis |
| MP-5 | Client ownership: the client owns final deliverables on payment; regulated decisions stay with the client's officers and board |
| MP-6 | Independence: no provider sells a service that impairs its independence as the buyer's auditor or certifier |
| MP-7 | No money custody: LawDocs never holds third-party fees; Razorpay Route (PA) or direct billing only |
| MP-8 | Neutral ranking: relevance by disclosed parameters; no paid placement for regulated professions; sponsored OFF |
| MP-9 | One record: every engagement is part of the client's LDC record and export (Appendix M) |
| MP-10 | Delight, measured: response times, first-time-right deliverables and CSAT are tracked and published only as evidenced aggregates |
