## 3. Operations Hub v1.0 constraints that bind LDC commerce (read 25 Oct 2026)
- OH8 is the group's commercial system of record: catalogue, price books, quotes, orders, subscriptions, entitlements (signed JWS), invoices (GST, e-invoice), Razorpay account per selling entity, dunning, revenue recognition. Products compute no price or tax; product billing pages call `POST /v1/billing/quotes`, `POST /v1/billing/orders`, `GET /v1/billing/invoices`, `POST /v1/usage-disputes`; daily `GET /v1/entitlements` reconciliation by 06:00 IST; products keep the last valid signed entitlement 30 days if the Hub is unreachable.
- OH8.2 streams: ONE_TIME_SERVICE, IMPLEMENTATION, SUBSCRIPTION, MONTHLY_RENTAL, PAYG, PROFESSIONAL_SERVICES, MANAGED_SERVICES, DATA_MIGRATION, TRAINING, PASS_THROUGH, PERPETUAL_LICENCE_AMC, MARKETPLACE_REFERRAL, ADVERTISING_SPONSORSHIP, LSP_FEES; legality gate DRAFT→LEGAL_REVIEW→TAX_REVIEW→OWNER_DECISION→APPROVED→ACTIVE→SUSPENDED/RETIRED; hard deny list includes "advocate listing or referral fees".

- OH8.9: trials are sandbox with synthetic data and never auto-convert; ptstdm.co.in-style funnels (countdown, anchor prices, unverifiable testimonials) are prohibited; claims gate (OH14.6, API `GET /v1/claims-gate/check`).

## 4. Payments (Razorpay)
- Subscriptions update (docs, read 25 Oct 2026): immediate or end-of-cycle; immediate update prorates (new plan cost for remaining days vs unused value of old plan; higher → invoice, if charge fails update does not happen; lower → credit note and automatic refund); prorated difference ≥ ₹0.50; only `authenticated`/`active` subscriptions updatable; **for subscriptions created with domestic cards only the offer can be updated** — so plan changes on card subscriptions require cancel-and-recreate or an OH8-managed invoice (design point); `subscription.updated` webhook. https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/subscriptions/update
- Route (marketplace split/linked accounts, transfers on hold) exists: https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/route/ — Razorpay acts as the authorised payment aggregator; the platform never holds funds.

## 6. Professional-conduct constraints
- **ICAI** Ethical Standards Board announcement 23 Oct 2019: members in practice may **not** list themselves with online application-based service-provider aggregators; violation of Clauses (6) and (7) of Part I of the First Schedule, CA Act 1949 (solicitation / advertising), even if no services are actually provided through it. Permitted: electronic trade directories under Code of Ethics conditions (alphabetical, "Chartered Accountants" heading, normal type, no undue prominence). Clause (2) Part I First Schedule: no sharing of fees with non-members; Clause (3): no acceptance of share of profits of professional work of a non-member. https://taxguru.in/chartered-accountant/cas-cannot-list-with-application-based-service-provider-aggregators-icai.html [VERIFY latest ICAI position 2026]
- **BCI** Rules Part VI Ch II r.36 (no solicitation/advertising; Schedule fields permitted on websites), fee-sharing bar; *Sulekha.com New Media Pvt Ltd v P.N. Vignesh* SLP (C) 25794/2024 pending — SC issued notice to BCI on scope of online listings; BCI March 2025 directives against unethical advertising and legal influencers. https://www.verdictum.in/court-updates/supreme-court/sulekhacom-new-media-private-limited-v-pn-vignesh-ors-special-leave-to-appeal-c-nos-257942024-1557830 ; https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/03/17/bci-condemns-warns-against-unethical-legal-advertising-advocates-legal-influencers-issues-directives/
