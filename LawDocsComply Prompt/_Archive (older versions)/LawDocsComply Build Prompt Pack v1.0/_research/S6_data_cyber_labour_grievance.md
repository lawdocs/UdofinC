## Data protection
- **DPDP Rules 2025** — notified by MeitY 13 Nov 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E) [VERIFY number]). Commencement: Rules 1, 2, 17–21 immediately; Rule 4 (Consent Manager registration) after 1 year (13 Nov 2026); Rules 3, 5–16, 22, 23 after 18 months (13 May 2027). Rule 6 minimum safeguards (encryption/obfuscation/masking/tokenisation, access control, logging & monitoring, backups, log + personal data retention ≥ 1 year, processor contracts). Rule 7 breach: intimate DPB and each affected Data Principal without delay; detailed report to DPB within 72 hours of becoming aware. Rule 8 + Third Schedule: erase when purpose served; e-commerce/gaming/social media above thresholds — 3 years from last engagement; 48-hour pre-erasure notice. Rule 13 SDF: India-based DPO reporting to Board, independent data auditor, DPIA + audit every 12 months, algorithmic due diligence. Rule 4: Consent Manager — Indian company, net worth ≥ ₹2 crore, records 7 years. Rule 15 cross-border: Central Government may restrict by notification. Sources: https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1708164/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified ; https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-in/alerts-hub/2025/11/digital-data-protection-act-rules-notified-by-meity.pdf ; https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/JSA-Prism-InfoTech-November-2025-DPDP-Rules.Final_.pdf .
