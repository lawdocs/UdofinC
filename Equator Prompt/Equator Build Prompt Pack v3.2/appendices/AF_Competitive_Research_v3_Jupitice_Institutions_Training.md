### AF.0.2 Evidence tags (Appendix T §0.2 tags, applied consistently)

| Tag | Meaning here | Public use |
|---|---|---|
| **[V]** | Verified on a primary or official page opened on the date shown (regulator, court, statute, an institution's own published schedule or rules) | Only through a Claims Register entry with URL and `verified_on` (NN-17) |
| **[V2]** | Reputable secondary source (law-firm note, press report) — research files label these [S] | Only attributed, through the Claims Register |
| **[C]** | Company self-description (own site, press release, sponsored or company-supplied article) | Never as fact |
| **[3P]** | Third-party aggregator or directory (FitGap, TechIndex, Gartner listing, reseller listing) | Never |
| **[K]** | Well-documented rule or practice, not re-fetched | Internal only |
| **[U]** | Not confirmed, not accessible, or inferred | Never |
| **[R]** | Recommendation (not a fact) | — |

**Separation rule.** Every statement about a company's scale, quality, recognition, certification or outcomes is **[C]** unless a regulator or court page confirms it. An institution's *own published fee schedule or rules* is primary evidence of what it charges or requires (**[V]**), not of how well it performs. **Institution-reported operating statistics** (case counts, hearing days, appointment shares, diversity percentages) are tagged **[C]** even when published on the institution's own site, and are cited only attributed ("ICC reports …").

**Comparative-advertising rule (Appendix T §0.3, binding through NN-17).** No Equator page names a competitor or compares itself unless every factual element links to a benchmark entry with tag V or V2 verified within 90 days. Equator never comments publicly on Jupitice's or any competitor's figures ([RA] F6 implication).

### AF.1.6 Terms, privacy and portability (as published, [C])

Terms cite the IT (Intermediaries Guidelines) Rules **2011**, superseded by the IT Rules 2021; the governing-law paragraph cross-referenced is missing; changes may be made at any time without notice; perpetual sublicensable licence over user content and brand material; data-loss risk placed on the user; accounts may be deleted without warning; no general refund policy. The Privacy Policy (effective 4 Nov 2022) names no statute (no DPDP Act), collects PAN, TIN, Aadhaar and users' contacts' details, shares data with advertising and analytics partners, has no portability right and no named Grievance Officer or DPO; cookie consent is implied by use [AF-S14]. **No export function** for parties, neutrals or institutions was described — the clearest anti-lock-in opening for Equator (NN-37).
