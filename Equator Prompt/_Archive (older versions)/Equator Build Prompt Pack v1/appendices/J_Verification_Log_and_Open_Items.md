## J.1 Method
1. All 514 flags were extracted with their context and grouped into six research areas: domestic ADR (140), international/MSME/consumer/stamp (47), financial sector — RBI/SEBI (63), insurance/workplace/family/education (82), data/technology/telecom (129), tax and fee benchmarks (53).

3. Seven editors then applied every resolution to the pack, applied cross-cutting corrections wherever they occurred (flagged or not), and reviewed every file end to end for errors and inconsistencies. The master prompt §15–§16 records the binding cross-file resolutions and management decisions.
