## Most important findings
1. **Singapore Convention Art. 1(3)(b)** excludes settlements "recorded and enforceable as an arbitral award". Converting an iMSA into a consent award or a s.74 settlement gives up the Convention route, so conversion and Art. 4 attestation are alternatives. A s.74 settlement is the weakest route abroad (J2-233/239/241/238).
2. **Status row verified on the UNCITRAL page:** 25 parties; UAE acceded 8 Oct 2026 (in force 8 Apr 2027); Mauritius ratified 17 Aug 2026 (in force 17 Feb 2027); India signed 7 Aug 2019, not ratified. Pack row correct.
3. **SC Draft AI Regulations 2026** (primary text, SC notice 03.06.2026). Draft Reg 43(3) requires a **signed declaration in the Annexure I format**, not just a stamp. Not yet notified; build the export behind a flag (J2-031/037/038).
4. **RB-IOS 2026:** an authorised representative may file, but **not an advocate** unless personally aggrieved (RBI FAQ) (J2-022).
5. **UP Tenancy Act 2021 partly struck down** (Allahabad HC, 2026:AHC:177234-DB, Aug 2026). EXC-27 must flag UP as unsettled (J2-013/284).
6. **COGSA 2025:** the time bar is Schedule **Art. III para 6(c)**; there is no 6bis. In force 10 Sep 2025 (S.O. 4082(E)). Edits for V6 and G LR-INT-023 (J2-235).
7. **NCLT/NCLAT format seed is wrong:** NCLAT r.19(1) and NCLT r.158 require double spacing, left margin 5 cm and right margin 2.5 cm, not the SC values (J2-029).
8. **BCI r.36 (2008 amendment):** permitted listing data is name and contacts; enrolment details; Bar Association; qualifications; areas of practice. The pack's advocate directory adds "languages" and "courts", which are not on the list (J2-090).
9. **UNCITRAL Model Law 2018 Art. 5(2):** the 30-day period runs from when the invitation is **sent**, and rejection is the inviting party's election. The pack said "delivery" (J2-297).
10. Also closed: ISA s.213 omission (Act 37 of 2025), BNSS ss.359/514, IR Code s.57(3), MSCS s.85A, hit-and-run amounts, EU ODR closure, DoE OM number, NALSA 15100, SHe-Box, Waqf s.83(9).
