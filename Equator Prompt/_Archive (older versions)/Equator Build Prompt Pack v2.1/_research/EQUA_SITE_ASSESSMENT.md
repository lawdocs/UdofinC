# Equa Site: Reuse Assessment for Equator Resolution

Basis: EQUA_SITE_EXTRACT.md. R = reusable; X = outdated or risky; G = gap.

**Cross-cutting**
- X: "Singapore | New Delhi | New York | London" offices are unverifiable, and there is no address, CIN or named officer (CPA 2019 s.2(28); Companies Act s.12(3)(c)).
- X: "Court" branding (Private ADR Courts, court.equa.law, "ADR judge"), qualified only on the Disclaimer page.
- X: Legacy vdrc.io contacts, live blanks, drafting notes (Rules 10.4, 15 "Decide").

**1. Institutional Rules**
- R: Dual entry route; electronic deemed service, including WhatsApp/Telegram; 7-day consultation; Case Manager; three-arbitrator mechanics; disclosure declarations; NDAs; data-protection directions; cost heads; New Delhi seat.
- X: The award is "rendered by Equa". The 7-day challenge conflicts with A&C s.13 (15 days, decided by the tribunal). The cl.16 waiver needs checking against s.42B.
- G: Pleadings (s.23(4)), the s.29A timeline, interim measures, award and settlement (ss.30–31), language, governing law, substitution of neutrals, and Mediation Act 2023 rules (ss.18, 22, 27; check notification).

**2. Neutrals' Code (strongest)**
- R: Continuing disclosure, 12-month cooling-off, confidentiality exceptions, withdrawal for unconscionable outcomes, fee and refund-on-withdrawal rules, eight international credits.
- G: Mediation-centric. Add the arbitrator Fifth/Seventh Schedules (s.12) and publish the Reviewer and Conduct Assessment Process.

**3. Privacy Policy**
- R: Purpose list, data inventory, CSV/JSON portability, third-party-data warranty.
- X: GDPR-only; the T&C cites only the IT Act. **No DPDP Act 2023**: ss.5–6 notice and consent, s.8 safeguards and breach intimation, s.9 children, ss.11–13 rights, DPDP Rules 2025.
- X: Collecting CVV breaches RBI card-storage rules. Aadhaar as a default ID is risky (Aadhaar Act s.29). AI profiling needs consent.
- G: Retention, a named Grievance Officer, a cookie policy.

**4. Terms & Conditions**
- R: E-acceptance (1.4), "administrative services only" (8.4), neutral non-endorsement (11.4), IP, termination.
- X: Blanks (3.1, 6.1–6.3); no cl.17. The fees-paid cap that also excludes direct loss risks an unfair-contract finding (CPA s.2(46)). LawDocs is named as tech provider without a DPA.
- G: IT Rules 2021 r.3; E-Commerce Rules 2020 r.4 grievance officer (48-hour acknowledgement, one-month resolution); jurisdiction clause.

**5. Data Security**
- R: AWS Mumbai residency, MFA, 3-hourly backups, 24/7 monitoring.
- X: Unevidenced SOC 2/HIPAA/ISO badges; AWS attestations presented as Equa's own; ISO 9001 called an ISMS.
- G: CERT-In Directions 2022 (6-hour reporting, 180-day logs), SPDI r.8, a DPDP breach procedure.

**6–7. Pricing, Refund and Fee Schedule**
- R: Graded fees: Arbitrator A ₹10,000 / B ₹5,000; Mediator A ₹8,000 / B ₹4,000 / C ₹2,000; ₹1,000 registration and per-session case management; 3-hour session; effective-hearing rule; deposit before session; worked example.
- X: "Non-refundable" with no timeline, mode or cancellation window, and Refund cl.2 vs cl.3 are inconsistent (E-Commerce r.4(10)). No GST.
- X: The "Failure Report" is not a s.12A Commercial Courts Act non-starter report.

**8. Disclaimer**
- R: The "Court" qualifier, no-legal-services statement, good-faith exclusion.

**9. ADR Clauses**
- R: Arbitration, Mediation and Med-Arb templates, placement notes, 11-point checklist.
- G: No mediation time-box; promised negotiation, multi-tier and expert-determination templates are absent.
- G: Lender and consumer clauses need review against *Perkins Eastman* / *CORE v ECI-SPIC* (2024), the RBI Fair Practices Code and the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021.

**10/15. How It Works and Resolve**
- R: 10-step flow, Claim (13-point) and Defence (16-point) checklists, 15-day reply, withdrawal at any time.
- X: "Decision posted on website" (A&C s.42A). "Silence is acceptance" (coercive, wrong in law). "Produce before any Court and execute" for mediated settlements (overstated).
- X: "Where there is a right, there is always a remedy" and "surprisingly correct" AI imply guaranteed outcomes. Staff outcome analysis raises Advocates Act s.29/33 issues.
- X: Domestic-violence, custody, criminal cheque-bounce and rent-control matters are not freely arbitrable.
- X: SmartExecution escrow needs a regulated escrow agent (PSS Act 2007).

**11–12. Accreditation and Registration**
- R: Grade matrix (A eminent; B 10+ years; Mediator C graduate with 3+ years), seven criteria, knowledge requirements, recognised trainers (IMI, SIMC, IICA and others), equal-access statement.
- X: Unpublished "bylaws" and "Advisory Board". "Train with us to qualify" is a conflict of interest.
- G: EQP/QAP details, fees, renewal, CPD, Mediation Council of India alignment.

**13. Private ADR Courts (highest risk)**
- X: Independent "courts" under unpublished "Procedural Rules", with self-set fees and unevidenced retired-judge claims. Re-scope as an accredited panel or white-label case management.

**14. Council and Secretariat**
- R: Four-council model and Terms of Reference.
- X/G: The Advisory Council "amends the Rules" with no constitution, quorum or term. No Registrar is named. Needs a charter that separates the owner from neutral administration.

**16. Grievance Portals and Accessibility**
- R: Consumer-ombudsman flow (internal first, external after 60 days, early-termination grounds, mediate then recommend); WCAG 2.0 AA.
- G: No mapping to RBI IGRM, the SEBI ODR circular (2023) or E-Commerce r.4. Accessibility needs WCAG 2.1/2.2, RPwD Act 2016 ss.40/46 and GIGW.
