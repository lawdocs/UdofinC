## Key findings
1. **IR Code s.42 (claim made by the other AI): correct but out of date.** The enacted s.42(1)–(8) on India Code matches the pack. The prescribed form now exists for the Central sphere: Form V under r.20 of the IR (Central) Rules 2026 (G.S.R. 342(E), 8 May 2026). Form V needs the arbitrator's written consent and goes to the conciliation officer and to the Secretary, MoLE. State forms vary: UP and RJ rules are reported final, MH is still a draft, and DL and KA are unconfirmed. Keep the flag OFF per State until the state_pack row is verified. The Central sphere can go live after counsel approves the Form V template.
2. **POSH s.11(4) (claim made by the other AI): correct.** The Gazette text says only that the inquiry "shall be completed within a period of ninety days". It gives no start point. The DoPT OM of 22 Dec 2016 (government servants only) caps the inquiry at 90 days. Keep the stricter default (count from complaint receipt) pending counsel.
3. **s.83 re-skilling fund: pack wrong.** Under r.37(1) the employer remits 15 days' wages to the fund within **10 days** of retrenchment. Under r.37(2) the fund credits the worker within **45 days**. Add an employer clock for the 10 days.
4. **IR Code s.53 verified:** s.53(1) proviso (2-year bar), s.53(5) (45 days, or 14 after a s.62 notice) and s.53(6) (90 days from receipt of the failure report). Under r.23(9) the Form II application runs 90 days "from the date of the report", which is earlier than receipt. Equator policy: compute from the report date.
5. **CoSS gratuity:** the 30-day payment period is s.56(3) and interest on delay is s.56(4). The s.53(2) part-year rule (more than 6 months counts as a year) is verified. Fixed-term workers qualify after 1 year under IR Code s.2(o) proviso (c), which is statute, not "Government communication". No s.53(3) ceiling notification and no s.56(4) interest-rate notification were found. The ₹20 lakh ceiling (S.O. 1420(E)) is plausibly saved by CoSS s.164(2)(a). Status: provisional/watch.
6. **Also verified:** IR Code s.57(1), (3) (who a settlement binds); IR (Central) Rules r.4 / Form I (settlement memorandum); CoSS s.89 (registration of compensation agreements); CoSS s.82 (2-year EC claim limit, condonable); CoSS s.51(1) (3-year EI Court limit); maternity is Ch. VI, ss.59–72.
7. **UGC Equity Regulations 2026:** the SC order of 29 Jan 2026 keeps them in abeyance, so the 2012 Regulations apply. The stay continued at the 20 Aug 2026 hearing. Watch.
8. **AICTE 2019 timelines (secondary sources only):** SGRC 15 days from the complaint; appeal 15 days. No Ombudsperson decision period was found.

## Errors found beyond my items

- **GRC numbering is wrong.** The 10-member cap is **s.4(4)** (pack says s.4(2)). The 30-day period is **s.4(6)** (pack says s.4(7)), including WC-06. Edits are in J-100 and J2-213.
- **V5 gratuity row:** the claimant appeal is cited as "Central Rule 33(4)(a) 180 days". The Code's s.56(8) gives 60 days, extendable by 60. Have counsel reconcile.
