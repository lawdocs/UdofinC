## Most important findings
1. **Calling-hours rule is in force now, not from 2027 (J-056, corrected).** RBI/2022-23/108 (12 Aug 2022) has a window of 08:00–19:00. It now sits in NBFC RBC Directions 2025 para 100. The Third Amendment (RBI/2026-27/230) deletes paras 89–100 only from 1 Jan 2027. LR-RBI-005's status "NOT_IN_FORCE" is wrong.
2. **Microfinance window missing from the pack.** Para 91(2) sets **09:00–18:00** for microfinance loans until 31 Dec 2026. `FIN_CONTACT_WINDOW` must carry a microfinance variant.
3. **Recovery-agency risk (J-057/J-095/J-108, counsel).** Para 6(11A) defines a "recovery agency" as any entity engaged by an NBFC under an outsourcing arrangement to help recover dues, "whatever the contract calls it". Para 100A also covers normal-course collection. If a lender pays Equator to run negotiation or offer relay on defaulted accounts, Equator may be a recovery agency. That brings IIBF certification, website disclosure and 1-day notice duties. **Launch-blocking for lender-paid FIN negotiation programmes until counsel rules.**
4. **Wrong circular number (J-094/J2-114).** The companion CIC customer-service circular is **RBI/2023-24/73** (DoR.FIN.REC.49), not /71. The error appears at P06:558 and V1:180.
5. **CIC compensation apportionment (J2-268, corrected).** The RBI/2023-24/72 Annex starts the CI's 21 days from the date the CIC informs the CI (for complaints filed with a CIC). It assigns final-delivery delay to the CI and splits multi-CI delay pro rata. Use Annex Cases 1–7 as test vectors.
6. **Bureau codes (J-089).** UCRF v3.74 Tag 22: 03 Settled, 04 Post (WO) Settled, 13 Post Write Off Closed. No RBI rule compels "closed". The CICRA s.19 accuracy duty limits any promise to report "closed".
7. **Instruments verified:**
   - Gold/Silver Directions: RBI/2025-26/47. Valuation is the lower of the 30-day average and the previous-day close (IBJA or SEBI-regulated exchange). For NBFCs: RBC paras 42–75.
   - Co-Lending Directions: RBI/DOR/2025-26/139. RBI does not fix the SPOC; it is whoever the loan agreement names (para 13).
   - Pre-payment Directions: RBI/2025-26/64 para 5. For NBFCs: RBC paras 33–34.
   - Document release: RBC paras 35–41 (provisional).
   - Credit cards: the 2022 MD is replaced for NBFCs and HFCs by RBI/DOR/2025-26/348, which keeps closure within 7 working days or ₹500 per calendar day (para 19) and the 30-day billing-dispute rule (para 25).
8. **RB-IOS 2026** defines no "day"; HFCs are excluded (cl.1(3)(b)) and the FAQ points to CEPC.
9. **s.31A (J-053/J-058).** s.31A(5) voids pre-dispute "costs in any event" clauses. Equator templates must not contain one.
10. **Limitation (J2-105):** mortgage-secured money is 12 years (Art. 62), not 3.
