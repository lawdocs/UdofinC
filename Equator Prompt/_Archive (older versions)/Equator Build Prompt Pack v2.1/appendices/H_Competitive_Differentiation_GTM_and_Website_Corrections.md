**Read-across (R1 §5).** No competitor combines appointment-defensible lender intake, native e-stamp + e-sign, settlement-money collection, regulator-mapped grievance-to-ODR escalation and a published fee card. That combination is Equator's lane. Credibility gaps Equator must close before claiming parity: SEBI/MII empanelment (all seven Indian peers hold it), panel depth (Sama and Jupitice each claim 3,000+ neutrals [company claims]), ISO 27001 (Webnyay certified), and an advisory council (Presolv360: former CJI U.U. Lalit, Justice B.N. Srikrishna, Justice K. Kannan).
