## 5. Know your rights (borrower panel)

- Recovery agents **cannot** threaten, abuse, or contact you at odd hours — that breaches the RBI Fair Practices Code.
- If your lender does not resolve a complaint within **30 days**, you can escalate **free** to the **RBI Integrated Ombudsman** at cms.rbi.org.in.
- A settlement must report your account **accurately** to the credit bureaus, and does not by itself bar you from future credit.
- Digital lenders must give you a **Key Fact Statement**, a **cooling-off** period, and a **grievance officer** (RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025).

*If you are ever in immediate danger from a recovery agent, contact the police.*

## 9. FAQ

**Is this the same as the RBI Ombudsman?** No. Equator is a neutral mediation and settlement institution. We complement the Ombudsman, the DRT and the courts — and we can mediate your settlement in parallel with an Ombudsman complaint.

**Will a settlement hurt my credit score?** A settlement is reported accurately as "settled/closed". It clears the outstanding dispute and, under the RBI framework, does not by itself bar you from future credit.

**Is it confidential?** Yes — mediation is confidential and without prejudice; what you say cannot be used against you in court, subject to the legal exceptions.

**How long does it take?** Most loan settlements complete in about 45–60 days; a cheque-bounce settlement closes on the next court/Lok Adalat date; the Ombudsman route follows the 30-day grievance window.

**What does it cost?** Fees are low and volume-friendly, with concessions for small and consumer matters. The OTS estimator shows the settlement figure up front.

**Is the settlement binding?** Yes — enforceable as a court decree (Mediation Act, s.27), or recorded in a Lok Adalat as a final, non-appealable award.
