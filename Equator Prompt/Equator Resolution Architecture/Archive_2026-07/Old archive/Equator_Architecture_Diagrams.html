5 · NBFC ↔ Borrower Escalation Ladder (SEBI SMART-ODR pattern)

High-volume, clause-driven. Bulk intake feeds a conciliation-first ladder; round-robin allocation to empanelled neutrals; awards/settlements flow to recovery.

6 · Enterprise Grievance & POSH Flow (SHe-Box pattern)

Single-window intake auto-routes to the Internal Committee; the 90-day POSH clock and strict confidentiality apply; unresolved matters hand off to EDRS for neutral resolution.

7 · Deployment Topology (cloud, India-first, multi-AZ)

Primary Indian region with multi-AZ HA; a second Indian region for DR. Public edge (CDN/WAF/gateway) fronts private service and data subnets. Global expansion replicates per residency zone.

8 · Core Data Model (ERD — central entities)

The relational heart. Full schema (60+ tables, ~190 columns) is in the Forms & Database Specification. Crow's-foot lines show one-to-many (fork) relationships.
