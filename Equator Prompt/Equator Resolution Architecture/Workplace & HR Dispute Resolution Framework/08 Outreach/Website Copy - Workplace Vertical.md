## 6. Know your rights (employees)

- Your **statutory dues** (gratuity, PF, ESI) must be paid — they cannot be settled away.
- A **sexual-harassment complaint** goes to the Internal Committee, stays confidential, and can never be diverted or based on money.
- A **grievance** can go to your establishment's Grievance Redressal Committee under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020.
- If there is a **criminal act or a threat to your safety**, you can also approach the police.

## 8. FAQ

**Do you handle sexual-harassment complaints?** POSH complaints belong to the employer's Internal Committee. We *support* the Committee — trained external members, secure confidential case management, and lawful s.10 conciliation at the complainant's request — but we never replace it, never base conciliation on money, and never handle the criminal aspect.

**Can a settlement waive my gratuity or PF?** No. Statutory dues are paid, not waived; a settlement records their proper payment.

**Is it confidential?** Yes — mediation is confidential and without prejudice; POSH matters are additionally protected under s.16 of the POSH Act.

**How long does it take?** Most wage/F&F and separation matters settle in weeks. POSH inquiries follow the statutory 90-day clock, run by the Internal Committee.

**Is the settlement binding?** Yes — enforceable as a court decree (Mediation Act, s.27).
