# Equator Resolution — Workplace & HR Dispute Resolution Framework
### Contents & how to use this folder

This folder is the complete content and product package for Equator Resolution's **workplace & HR** dispute vertical — employment, wage, full-and-final, termination, separation and grievance disputes resolved by institutional mediation and conciliation, plus **POSH support** for employers' Internal Committees. It mirrors the *Marital*, *Commercial* and *Financial & Loan* frameworks in this workspace.

**Prepared:** July 2026 · **Status:** Draft for legal sign-off before go-live · An Infrabyx Group venture · www.equatorresolution.com

> **Not legal advice.** Everything here is a working draft. Have qualified counsel and a Data Protection Officer review the legal content and statutory references — the **POSH Act, 2013**, the four **Labour Codes** (in force from 21 November 2025), the **Mediation Act, 2023** and the **DPDP Act, 2023** — before the platform goes live or any document is used with a real party.

> **A note on POSH.** Equator **supports** employers' Internal Committees (trained external members, secure confidential case management, IC training) and facilitates the statutory **s.10 conciliation at the aggrieved woman's request only, never on a monetary basis**. It does **not** replace the Internal Committee and does not handle the criminal aspect. Safety and the complainant's autonomy come first.

## What's in each folder

**01 Master Blueprint** — the 51-page master document (Word + PDF), Parts A–H: executive summary and vision; the legal landscape (the Mediation Act; the POSH Act — IC, external member, s.10, 90-day inquiry, s.16 confidentiality; the four Labour Codes now in force; individual employment and contract disputes; the mediable/non-mediable boundary); the platform design (taxonomy, dropdowns, onboarding, workflow, either-party initiation, neutral/IC selection, sealed POSH mode, case-manager playbook, SLAs, settlement/F&F, enforcement, permutations); governance, fees, risk, roadmap and KPIs; global best practice (ACAS, EEOC, Fair Work); the technology layer (AI Dispute Explorer, ODR, e-Sign, e-Stamp, HRMS/IC integration); annexures and model clauses; and **Part H — the full text of all 20 forms**.

**02 Developer Handoff** — a 13-sheet Excel workbook: dropdown lists, dispute taxonomy, form-field schema, neutral & IC filters, workflow states, SLAs and statutory clocks, API endpoints, integrations, PII/data map (with sealed POSH handling), AI features and KPIs, plus a live sample-intake sheet.

**03 Workflow Diagrams** — eight diagrams as PNG (for documents) and editable SVG: the workplace dispute lifecycle, route triage, the POSH Internal-Committee process, employment mediation, full-and-final + escrow, grievance redressal, enforcement/timeline, and the AI Dispute Explorer logic.

**04 Archive - Forms Pack (standalone)** — all 20 forms (F-W01 to F-W20) as individual Word documents, from onboarding and the request for resolution through the POSH complaint and s.10 conciliation, the IC inquiry record and external-member appointment, wage/F&F and separation settlements, the GRC referral, escrow, and the F&F statement. The same text is embedded in the Blueprint (Part H).

**05 Board Deck** — a 12-slide board & investor presentation (PowerPoint): the problem, four regulatory tailwinds, the solution, differentiators, coverage, a dedicated POSH slide, technology, global best practice, business model, roadmap and the ask.

**06 Calculators** — three Excel tools with live, verified formulas: the **Full & Final (F&F) Settlement Calculator** (earnings less deductions, with gratuity), the **Gratuity Calculator** ((15 × last wages × years) / 26, with eligibility and cap), and the **Notice Pay & Severance / Retrenchment Calculator** (notice-pay in lieu and IR-Code retrenchment compensation).

**07 Operations** — the **Neutral & IC Toolkit and SOP Runbooks** (Word: mediator/conciliator scripts, a POSH & Internal-Committee runbook, case-manager and escrow runbooks, ethics and checklists) and the **Compliance & Security Workbook** (Excel: POSH & Labour-Code regulatory tracker, security controls with sealed POSH access, DPDP breach-response runbook).

**08 Outreach** — the **employee & employer leaflet** (Word) and the **website copy & SEO pack** (Markdown).

**09 Interactive Prototype** — the **Workplace Dispute Explorer** (a single HTML file). Open in any browser: it walks an employee or employer through their dispute, recommends the route (mediation / POSH Internal Committee / grievance committee / police), explains their rights, and drafts a grievance, POSH complaint or request. Includes a rule-based AI assistant with POSH-safety handling. A demonstration only — it stores no data and gives no legal advice.

## Important scope note

Equator is a **neutral mediation, conciliation and settlement institution**. For sexual-harassment matters it **supports** the employer's Internal Committee and never replaces it, never bases conciliation on money, and never handles the criminal aspect. For collective or statutory industrial disputes it signposts the Industrial-Relations-Code machinery and mediates a settlement only where lawful. Statutory dues (gratuity/PF/ESI) are always paid, never waived. All statutory references and timelines must be confirmed by counsel as at the go-live date, including the progressive notification of the Labour-Code rules in the relevant State.
