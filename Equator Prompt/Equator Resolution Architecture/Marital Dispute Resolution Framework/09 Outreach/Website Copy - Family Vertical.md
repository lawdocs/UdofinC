## 2. How it works (4 steps)

1. **Reach out — either of you.** Husband or wife can start. Tell us what you need to resolve; we invite your spouse to join.
2. **Choose your mediator.** Pick a neutral, trained mediator together, or let us match you — by language, city and expertise.

## 6. FAQ

**Is what I say confidential?** Yes. Mediation is private and, by law, what you say cannot be used against you in court — the only exception is information about someone being at risk of harm.

**Do we have to be in the same room?** No. Sessions can be online, and the mediator can meet each of you separately.

**Is the outcome legally binding?** Yes. Once your Mediated Settlement Agreement is signed and registered under the Mediation Act, 2023, it is enforceable as a decree of a court.

**Can either of us start?** Yes — husband or wife. The other is formally invited to take part; participation is voluntary.

**What if we have children?** Their welfare guides everything. We help you build a detailed parenting plan a court will readily accept.

**We've filed police cases against each other. Can that be resolved?** Often, yes. Where the offence and the law allow, we help you close those cases lawfully as part of the settlement. Some serious offences cannot be closed simply by agreement.

**How long does it take?** The law allows up to 180 days; many matters settle well within that.

**What does it cost?** A clear, published schedule — far less than years of litigation. Fee support is available for those who cannot afford it.
