# Where the platform-wide documents moved to

The following documents apply to **the whole institution, not just the family vertical**, so they now live at:

`01 PLATFORM (institution-wide)/`

**Public Legal Pages/** — Terms of Service · Privacy Policy & DPDP Notice · Grievance Redressal Policy · Cookie & Consent Notice
