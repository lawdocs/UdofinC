# Equator Resolution — Marital & Family Dispute Resolution Framework

The complete design, legal, operations, technology and go-to-market package for Equator's
Family vertical — a Mediation Service Provider under the Mediation Act, 2023, built for
AI-assisted, fully-online (ODR) resolution with e-Sign, e-Stamp and court integration.
Law stated as of July 2026 (BNS/BNSS from 1 July 2024; DPDP Rules 2025).

## The digital journey
File online → AI-assisted intake & drafting → mediate online (ODR, video + caucus) → e-Sign
(Aadhaar/DSC) → e-Stamp (SHCIL) → register (s.20) → enforceable as a decree (s.27); with two-way
court referral and a certificate of attempted mediation where it doesn't settle.
