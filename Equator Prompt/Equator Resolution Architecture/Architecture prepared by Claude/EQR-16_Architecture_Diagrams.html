1 · System Context

Who and what the platform connects to: user personas (left), external systems and rails (right), and the client apps (bottom).

2 · Layered Architecture

Channels flow through one API gateway to bounded application services over a shared data & platform layer, with security, privacy, observability and AI governance cutting across every layer.

3 · Microservices Map

The application services grouped by concern. Each is independently deployable and stateless where possible.

4 · Case Lifecycle State Machine

The canonical case states. Each transition emits a domain event and may start or stop a compliance clock; side-states handle deficiency cure, execution and challenge.

5 · The Resolution Ladder

Four methods on one engine, differentiated by who decides and how the outcome is enforced.

6 · Company Complaint-to-Escalation Flow

A company resolves in-house first; on SLA breach the ticket escalates to Equator via API, becomes a case, and the outcome syncs back.

7 · Dispute Explorer (AI) Flow

The pre-litigation consultation: guided intake, optional document extraction, a grounded Claude call, and a viability read with a recommended next step.

8 · Payments, Escrow & Payout

One party payment is split at source into the neutral's fee and the platform fee; settlement money escrows and releases on performance; neutrals are paid out on the matching rail.

9 · Deployment Topology

Core services run across three availability zones in an Indian region for residency and resilience; a warm secondary region provides disaster recovery with strict RPO/RTO.

10 · High-level Entity-Relationship (core)

The core case aggregate and its neighbours. Full field-level schema is in EQR-12.
