## 2. Which mechanism? (mechanism finder)

- **Arbitration** — where your contract has an arbitration clause and the dispute is arbitrable. A private tribunal, a binding award.

- **MSME Samadhaan** — for micro and small suppliers chasing delayed payments: conciliation, then arbitration, with statutory interest.

## 6. FAQ

**Is the outcome binding?** An arbitral award is final and enforceable as a court decree (s.36); a mediated settlement is enforceable as a decree (s.27); foreign awards under the New York Convention.

**How long does it take?** Arbitration: award within 12 months of pleadings, +6 by consent (s.29A); fast-track 6 months. s.12A mediation: 3 months (+2). MSME: ~90 days.

**We have no arbitration clause — can we still use Equator?** Yes — via mediation, the mandatory s.12A route before a commercial suit, or MSME Samadhaan; and you can add the Equator clause to future contracts.

**Can everything be arbitrated?** No — insolvency, company oppression/mismanagement and some matters are non-arbitrable (Vidya Drolia) and go to the NCLT/court; the commercial layer can still be mediated.

**What does it cost?** Ad valorem fees scaled to the amount in dispute; our fee estimator gives an instant quote.

**Is it confidential?** Yes — the proceeding, submissions and outcome are confidential, save as needed for enforcement or by law.

**What about urgent relief?** Emergency-arbitrator relief and court interim measures (s.9) are available before the tribunal is constituted.
