# Equator Resolution — Commercial & Business Dispute Resolution Framework

The complete design, legal, operations, technology and go-to-market package for Equator's
**Commercial** vertical — an institutional centre for **arbitration, mediation and MSME
resolution** covering contract, commercial, MSME, company and transaction disputes.
Law stated as of July 2026 (Arbitration & Conciliation Act 1996 + draft 2024 Bill; Mediation
Act 2023; Commercial Courts Act s.12A; MSMED Act 2006; DPDP Act 2023 & Rules 2025).

## Contents

**01 Master Blueprint** — the core document (Parts A–H, ~60+ pages): strategy & opportunity;
legal framework (Arbitration Act; mediation & mandatory s.12A; MSME; company/NCLT; contract;
arbitrability (Vidya Drolia); interim & emergency arbitration; enforcement incl. New York &
Singapore Conventions); platform, taxonomy, dual-track workflow, neutral selection,

**02 Developer Handoff** — Excel (13 sheets): dropdowns, dispute taxonomy, form schemas

**03 Workflow Diagrams** — 10 diagrams (PNG + editable SVG): arbitration lifecycle, s.12A
mediation, MSME Samadhaan, arbitrability triage, neutral appointment, enforcement, escrow,

**04 Archive - Forms Pack (standalone)** — 20 forms (F-C01–F-C20) as individual .docx

**08 Outreach** — website copy + SEO (Markdown) and a business leaflet (Word).

## The digital journey
File online → AI triage (mechanism + arbitrability) → appoint neutral → resolve online (ODR)
→ e-Sign (Aadhaar/DSC) → e-Stamp (SHCIL) → award (s.31) / consent award (s.30) / MSA →
enforceable as a decree (s.36 / s.27); foreign awards under the New York Convention; with
two-way court/tribunal integration.
